The much‑anticipated Galactic Rail was delayed again today after an unseasonal migration of intergalactic snails occupied the primary hyperspace lane. Commuters reported “slow but determined” traffic as the snails refused to yield, citing their right‑of‑way according to the Celestial Transit Charter of Year 9. Officials say the next departure will occur as soon as the snails complete their weekly stretch, estimated sometime between five minutes and five millennia. A complimentary packet of stardust and a commemorative “I survived the snailway” sticker will be provided to all ticket holders.
